## Jac Living His Dream

Major League Baseball has announced that Jac Caglianone has accepted their invitation to participate in the 2026 Home Run Derby during All-Star Week in Philadelphia. Caglianone, who has hit 14 HRs this season, has recently had an offensive explosion. It is likely though that he is being asked less because of the amount of homers he’s hit and more because of the sheer power he has and the spectacle he can put on.

In June, Jac hit a home run to the cat-walk above the scoreboard in Tropicana Field. We hope to see him do similar things next week in Philly!
