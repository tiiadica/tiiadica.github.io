## Kansas City has taken a collective breath of relief; with that came a newfound confidence in their team to bring home a trophy

KC Current fans sat through 113 minutes of pure, heart pumping agony on Sunday until the ball finally found the net off of Michelle Cooper's boot in extra time. As someone who was there, all I had in my mind the whole time was the feeling of last year's quarterfinal against Gotham where we were trailing until the very last minute... followed by complete elation when we equalized at the theoretical buzzer... followed by complete heartbreak when we couldn't finish the job.

The Current never trailed in Sunday's quarterfinal against Angel City; but every second that we didn't score made it feel like we were trailing. It felt like it was only a matter of time that we conceded and then last year unfolded all over again.

But there was something different about this team from what we've seen all season. We weren't the same team that had only completed 6 clean sheets on the year. Major defensive contributions were made from the likes of Laney Rouse, Gabrielle Robinson, Kayla Sharples, Lo'eau Labonta; even Haley Hopkins and Temwa Chawinga were locking down valiant Angel City attacks. Backed up by yet another incredible performance from Lorena -- outside of one notable but ultimately uncostly flub -- it's clear this teams drive, passion, and desire to win, for each other, far outweighs what the regular season defensive numbers would tell you.

Now they have their eyes trained on Gotham FC again; but this time they're not a heavy favorite ready to breeze by their pedantically inferior opponent. No. This time they're the underdogs. The team that 2 months ago, many around the league and the city had written off because after an already disappointing and inconsistent season; they lost one of the few figures within the organization that they knew they could fully trust. Most teams would feel defeated, demoralized, and would lack the desire for a strong finish that was so desperately needed. Luckily, this team had strong veteran leadership, a coaching staff that earned new trust, and -- of course -- unbridled talent to carry them to the strongest finish in the league.

Gotham might still be favored on paper. But the numbers are underestimating the KC Current's superpower: health and the desire for revenge.

We break down the Angel City match, go over what needs to happen for the Current to be successful against Gotham, and more on this weeks podcast episode of Current Thoughts!
